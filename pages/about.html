Our Mission

Glorifying God, Growing in Christ, Giving to the World

Uyaranthamuthukkal is a Spirit-filled congregation committed to the Great Commission — making disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and teaching them to observe all that Christ commanded.

Founded on the unshakeable foundation of Scripture, we are a diverse family of believers from all walks of life, united by our love for Jesus Christ and our passion to see His Kingdom come on earth as it is in heaven.

  • Word-Centered — Every study and devotion is anchored in the Holy Bible.
  • Prayer-Driven — We believe in the power of persistent, faith-filled prayer.
  • Community-Focused — We love and serve one another and our surrounding community.
  • Mission-Minded — We actively support local and global missionary work.

Our Journey of Faith

How God has written our story through the years

Church Founded

A small group of 12 believers began gathering in a living room with one prayer — that God would build something eternal for His glory in this community.

2005

First Dedicated Sanctuary

After years of prayer and faithful giving, God provided the means to purchase and dedicate our first permanent church building. 150 members gathered for the inaugural worship.

2010

Tamil Nadu Outreach Launch

God opened doors for us to plant two daughter churches in rural Tamil Nadu, bringing the gospel to unreached villages and establishing vibrant local congregations.

2015

New Campus & Online Ministry

With a growing congregation of 800+, we expanded to a new facility and launched online services and this blog platform to reach believers across the globe.
